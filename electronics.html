Electronics

Electronics equipment is labeled TEAL.

With great power...

Electronics and coding training happens as requested by staff and community members. It is also the subject of some ATLAS classes and BTU workshops. See the BTU calendar for upcoming workshops.

See the Mischief Makers page to contact one of our community experts to get you started.

Electronics equipment and supplies

  • Soldering irons and accessories
  • Bench top power supplies
  • Soldering rework station
  • Digital oscilloscope
  • Arduino / Raspberry Pi ecosystem
  • Various power components
  • Various input components
  • Various output components
  • LilyPad
  • Prototyping supplies

Materials: BTU Lab is not a free supply store, but there are many ways to barter: displaying your work in BTU, tagging us on social media, offering workshops, volunteering to organize supplies, and more. If you need something, we ask that you give something in return.
