The tools and the talent

BTU Lab keeps lots of general tools on hand for all your inventing needs. We can accommodate dozens of people at once and are working on increasing project storage space.

General equipment

  • Knives
  • Cutting mats
  • Scissors (not for fabric)
  • Measuring tools
  • Various glues
  • Various screwdrivers
  • Zip ties
  • Plywood (laser safe)
  • Hot glue guns
  • Various tapes
  • Dremel tools
  • Drills / drivers
  • Various papers
  • 2D craft supplies
  • 3D craft supplies
  • Plastics (laser safe)
  • Various fasteners
  • Sandpaper
  • Sanding tools
  • Saws
  • Files
  • Chisels
  • Various fabrics
  • ...and more

Materials: BTU Lab is not a free supply store, but there are many ways to barter: displaying your work in BTU, tagging us on social media, offering workshops, volunteering to organize supplies, and more. If you need something, we ask that you give something in return.
