Rules & Policies

The one rule: be cool.

Ways to be cool

  • Respect others, their space, and their work.
  • Observe safety for you, others, and equipment.
  • Report problems with equipment.
  • Using slang or pop culture as an excuse for denigrating people based on race, age, gender, sexual orientation, physical ability, etc. is not for the BTU.
  • Report taking the last of something (glue sticks, for example).
  • Ask approval before removing any tool or material from the lab.
  • Protect surfaces from hot glue, paint, marmots, etc.
  • Keep common work areas clear of your stuff when you can.
  • Put things back where they belong, not just where you found them.
  • Let the staff know if anyone is not being cool.
  • Clear scraps off common work areas and sweep up scraps on the floor.
  • Use tools, materials and processes as intended. Get trained; that's why you're here.
  • Leave out anything else that negatively affects the BTU, ATLAS, CU, and Earth community.

Policies

  • BTU Lab observes all policies and codes of conduct of the University of Colorado.
  • Access to BTU will be removed for anyone repeatedly disregarding lab rules and policies or disrespecting others.

Materials: BTU Lab is not a free supply store, but there are many ways to barter: displaying your work in BTU, tagging us on social media, offering workshops, volunteering to organize supplies, and more. If you need something, we ask that you give something in return.
