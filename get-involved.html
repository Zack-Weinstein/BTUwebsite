Get Involved

BTU Lab runs on its community. Here are two ways to give back.

Teach a workshop

BTU Lab hosts all kinds of workshops. Whether you're a student, instructor, or local artist, come share your skills in two steps:

  1. Fill out the proposal form.
  2. Email atlasbtulab@gmail.com to let us know you did.

We'll get back to you with confirmation as soon as possible.

Support the lab

If you're interested in ways to support BTU Lab, contact lab director Zack Weaver at zaja9653@colorado.edu.

If you've visited and know a good thing when you see it, you can go directly to our giving page. Thank you from the whole BTU community.
